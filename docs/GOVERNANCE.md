# Project Governance 🏛️

LeakLens is a community-driven project. We follow a "Lazy Consensus" model to ensure rapid
development while maintaining high technical standards.

## Roles & Responsibilities

### 👥 Users

Anyone who uses LeakLens in their daily development. Users are encouraged to report bugs, request
features, and participate in [Discussions](https://github.com/dev-vikas-soni/leak-lens/discussions).

### ✍️ Contributors

Users who contribute code, documentation, or design.

* **Path to contribution**: Fork the repo, submit a PR.
* **Requirements**: PRs must pass all CI checks and follow
  the [Coding Standards](CONTRIBUTING.md#coding-standards).

### 🛡️ Maintainers

Contributors who have demonstrated long-term commitment and technical excellence.

* **Power**: They have "Merge" access and oversee architectural decisions.
* **Responsibility**: Reviewing community PRs and managing releases.
* **Promotion**: Maintainers are appointed by existing maintainers based on sustained contribution
  quality.

## Decision Making Process

1. **Transparency**: All technical decisions happen
   in [Issues](https://github.com/dev-vikas-soni/leak-lens/issues)
   or [Discussions](https://github.com/dev-vikas-soni/leak-lens/discussions).
2. **Lazy Consensus**: For minor changes (docs, bug fixes), a maintainer can merge if there are no
   objections within 48 hours of PR submission.
3. **Architectural Shifts**: Major changes (e.g., adding KMP support) require explicit approval from
   at least two maintainers.

## Conflict Resolution

Disagreements are resolved through civil technical discussion. If consensus cannot be reached, the
Project Lead (@dev-vikas-soni) has the final say.
