Reports Hilt components that inject a dependency with a narrower scope than their own, through an @Inject constructor or field (also through Provider<T> and dagger.Lazy<T>).

A @Singleton holding an @ActivityScoped object retains that object, and typically its Activity, for the lifetime of the process.

A scope may only depend on itself and its ancestors:

  • @Singleton → Singleton
  • @ActivityRetainedScoped → Singleton, ActivityRetained
  • @ViewModelScoped / @HiltViewModel → Singleton, ActivityRetained, ViewModel
  • @ActivityScoped → Singleton, ActivityRetained, Activity
  • @FragmentScoped → … Activity, Fragment
  • @ViewScoped → … Activity, View
  • @ServiceScoped → Singleton, Service

Leaks:


@ActivityScoped class Navigator @Inject constructor(private val activity: Activity)
@Singleton class Analytics @Inject constructor(private val navigator: Navigator)

Fix: inject the narrower dependency where it is used, pass it as a method argument, or deliberately widen its scope.
